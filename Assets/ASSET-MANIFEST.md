# MBB V2 — Landing Page Concept & Asset Manifest

## The concept (as described 2026-08-07)

Landing page for [brand name — TBD]. High-class stationery theme.

- Two buttons on the page, styled as invitation cards tucked into envelopes.
- Hover over a button → the invitation card animates slightly (e.g. rises/tilts out of the envelope).
- Each envelope shows a label — "Corporate" and "Brands" — printed on the visible edge of the card inside the envelope.
- Hover → the card pulls further out of the envelope, revealing a tagline: "click here to…" (tagline copy TBD).
- Click → takes the visitor to one of two different sides of the brand (Corporate vs. Brands) — what each side actually contains is still to be explained/defined.

Open questions to resolve before build:
- Final brand name for the landing page.
- The two taglines ("click here to...").
- What "Corporate" and "Brands" each lead to (page content/structure).

## Shared visual language across all assets
- Palette: sage/olive greens, cream/ivory paper, gold foil accents.
- Typography: serif small-caps / classic engraved style (e.g. "Corporate", "Brands").
- Motif: 8-point compass star, used both as a wax seal stamp and as a small divider/ornament on card fronts.
- Consistent soft studio lighting with a vignette background.
